This chapter presents VibVoice, an end-to-end multimodal speech enhancement system for earables, the ear-worn subset of HMWs. It instantiates the \textit{differentiation} dimension of user--environment coupling: VibVoice uses bone-conducted vibration sensed by an on-device IMU as a user-specific reference to separate the wearer's voice from microphone audio. The chapter describes the motivation, system design, and evaluation of VibVoice.
