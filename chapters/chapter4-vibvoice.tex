In this chapter, we present VibVoice, an end-to-end multi-modal speech enhancement system for commercial head-mounted wearables (HMWs) that fuses bone-conducted vibrations sensed by the on-device IMU with microphone audio to isolate the wearer's voice. VibVoice is the first system to enable bone-conduction–guided speech enhancement on off-the-shelf earphones and headsets with lightweight, on-device inference.
