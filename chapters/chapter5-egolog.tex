This chapter presents EgoLog, a mobile sensing framework for daily logging with audio and motion sensors. EgoLog can run on several mobile and wearable form factors, while the thesis emphasizes head-mounted wearables because they are suitable for day-long use and provide stable egocentric audio--motion observations. EgoLog selectively separates user-generated and environmental signals, then fuses activity cues with scenario context to achieve robust recognition in the wild.
