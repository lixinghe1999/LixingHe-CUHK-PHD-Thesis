This chapter presents CoHear, a cooperative hearing system that uses multiple earphone devices to enhance conversation clarity in challenging acoustic environments. CoHear models natural conversational interaction to identify participants, then combines audio inputs from participant-worn devices to improve speech intelligibility and suppress irrelevant sound.
The following sections describe the motivation, system design, implementation, and evaluation.
