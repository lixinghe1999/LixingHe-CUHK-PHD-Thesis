The chapter presents CoHear, a novel cooperative hearing system that leverages multiple devices to enhance conversation clarity in challenging environments. CoHear utilizes the natural interaction within the conversation and identify the participants. Then, it combines audio inputs from multiple devices worn by the participants to improve speech intelligibility and filter out the unwanted noise. 
In the following, we detail the introduction, system, implementation, and evaluation in individual sections.
