\begin{abstract}
  True Wireless Stereo (TWS) earphones are a key type of head-mounted wearable (HMW), which also includes VR/AR headsets and smart glasses. These smart devices are designed to be worn on the ears or head. Popular products like Apple AirPods have made earphones the largest segment in the wearable device market, with an estimated global shipment of 455 million units in 2024, reflecting an 11.2\% year-on-year growth.
Typically, there are two main types of sensors on earphones: microphones and inertial measurement units (IMUs). Note that these technologies are commonly used in all head-mounted wearables, not just earphones.
The sensing abilities of earphones are often limited because manufacturers prioritize delivering content, like spatial audio and 3D video. Therefore, this dissertation aims to enhance the sensing capabilities of earphones.
Unlike existing research that typically focuses on either the environment or the user, our work uniquely models both through the principle of \textit{user-environment coupling}. This principle recognizes that comprehensive understanding of human behavior requires modeling the dynamic interplay between individuals and their surroundings across three fundamental dimensions: the acoustic boundary between user-generated sounds and environmental noise, the contextual relationship between user activities and ambient scenarios, and the social fabric connecting multiple users through shared acoustic and spatial contexts. By leveraging earphones' unique capability for user-environment differentiation through multi-modal sensing, this dissertation demonstrates how earphones can serve as a comprehensive platform for human-centric sensing.

This dissertation specifically introduces \textit{VibVoice}, a novel speech enhancement system that leverages bone vibrations conducted through the skull, captured by accelerometers widely available in head-mounted wearables. Environmental noise can significantly interfere with voice-related applications on these devices, which represents one of the key interactions between humans and their surroundings.
We develop a lightweight multi-modal architecture featuring a two-branch encoder-decoder deep neural network that fuses high-level features from both audio and vibration modalities to reconstruct clean speech. To address the challenge of insufficient paired training data, we conduct extensive measurements of bone conduction effects to extract physical impulse responses for cross-modal data augmentation. Our evaluation demonstrates that VibVoice achieves up to 21\% improvement in PESQ and 26\% improvement in SNR over state-of-the-art baselines while requiring 72 times less paired data. A user study with 35 participants shows that 87\% prefer VibVoice over the baseline. Additionally, VibVoice achieves 4 to 31 times faster execution on mobile devices compared to baselines.

We then extend the sensing capabilities of earphones beyond vocal activities to fine-grained activity logging. We propose \textit{EgoLog}, a sensing system that enables fine-grained activity logging using existing sensors in commercial earphones. The system records both user activities and contextual scenarios, facilitating detailed behavior understanding.
EgoLog addresses three key challenges: limited recognition capability due to sparse sensor configurations, ineffective fusion for extracting ambient information, and interference from environmental noise. Our system architecture comprises a context-aware scenario recognition module and a spatial-aware activity detection module, integrated with other attributes through expert knowledge. Implemented on commercial earphones equipped with binaural microphones and an IMU, EgoLog achieves zero-shot fine-grained activity logging across up to 41 categories, outperforming strong baselines such as ImageBind-LLM by 38\% in F1-score.

Finally, we scale up our system into a multi-device collaborative paradigm. Specifically, we propose \textit{CoHear}, the first collaborative speech enhancement system that operates at the conversation level across multiple earphones. To enable real-time conversation enhancement, the system performs holistic modeling of all conversation participants and effectively extracts target speech from acoustic mixtures. CoHear makes two key contributions: 1) a conversation-driven network protocol that enables mobile, infrastructure-free coordination among earphone devices, and 2) a robust target conversation extraction model that leverages relay audio in a bandwidth-efficient manner. Evaluation through real-world experiments and simulations demonstrates that our conversation network achieves over 90\% accuracy in group formation, improves speech quality by up to 8.8 dB over state-of-the-art baselines, and maintains real-time performance on mobile devices. A user study with 20 participants confirms CoHear's superior performance and good usability compared to baselines.

In summary, this dissertation presents three systems that instantiate the user-environment coupling principle across acoustic, contextual, and social dimensions on HMWs with existing sensors. These systems provide not only detailed implementation but also bring new insights into the mobile sensing community, demonstrating how earphones can serve as a comprehensive platform for understanding users through their environmental context.

\end{abstract}

\begin{cabstract}
  真无线立体声（TWS）耳机是头显可穿戴设备（HMW）的一种重要类型，其他类型还包括虚拟现实/增强现实（VR/AR）头显和智能眼镜。这些智能设备设计为佩戴在耳朵或头部。以苹果AirPods为代表的热门产品使耳机成为可穿戴设备市场中最大的细分市场，预计2024年全球出货量将达到4.55亿台，同比增长11.2\%。

通常，头显可穿戴设备采用两种主要的感知技术：麦克风和惯性测量单元（IMU）。这些技术不仅限于耳机，而是广泛应用于所有头显可穿戴设备。然而，耳机的感知能力常常受限，因为制造商更专注于内容传递，例如空间音频和3D视频。因此，本论文旨在提升头显可穿戴设备的感知能力。

与现有耳机感知研究不同，我们的工作独特地通过\textit{用户-环境耦合}原理同时建模环境和用户。该原理认为，全面理解人类行为需要建模个体与其周围环境之间的动态交互，涵盖三个基本维度：用户生成声音与环境噪声之间的声学边界、用户活动与环境场景之间的上下文关系，以及通过共享声学和空间上下文连接多个用户的社交结构。通过利用耳机独特的多模态感知能力实现用户-环境区分，本论文展示了耳机如何成为以人为中心的综合感知平台。

本论文具体介绍了 VibVoice，这是一种新颖的语音增强系统，利用头显可穿戴设备中广泛可用的加速度计捕获通过颅骨传导的骨振动。环境噪声会显著干扰这些设备上的语音相关应用，这是人类与环境交互的关键方面之一。我们开发了一种轻量级多模态架构，采用双分支编码器-解码器深度神经网络，融合音频和振动两种模态的高级特征以重建干净语音。为了解决配对训练数据不足的挑战，我们广泛测量骨传导效应，提取物理冲激响应用于跨模态数据增强。评估结果表明，VibVoice 在 PESQ 指标上比最先进的基线提高了21\%，在信噪比（SNR）上提高了26\%，同时所需配对数据减少了72倍。一项包含35名参与者的用户研究显示，87\%的用户更偏好 VibVoice。此外，VibVoice 在移动设备上的执行速度比基线快4到31倍。

随后，我们将耳机的感知能力扩展到语音活动之外，实现细粒度活动记录。我们提出了 EgoLog，一种使用商用耳机现有传感器实现细粒度活动记录的感知系统。该系统记录用户活动和上下文场景，促进详细的行为理解。EgoLog 解决了三个关键挑战：稀疏传感器配置导致的识别能力有限、提取环境信息的低效融合，以及环境噪声干扰。我们的系统架构包括上下文感知的场景识别模块和空间感知的活动检测模块，通过专家知识与其他属性集成。在配备双耳麦克风和IMU的商用耳机上实现的 EgoLog，实现了多达41个类别的零样本细粒度活动记录，F1分数比 ImageBind-LLM 等强基线高出38\%。

最后，我们将系统扩展为多设备协作范式。具体来说，我们提出了 CoHear，这是首个在多只耳机之间进行对话级语音增强的协作系统。为了实现实时对话增强，该系统对所有对话参与者进行整体建模，并有效从声学混合物中提取目标语音。CoHear 做出了两个关键贡献：1）一种对话驱动的网络协议，实现耳机设备之间移动、无基础设施的协调；2）一种鲁棒的目标对话提取模型，以带宽高效的方式利用中继音频。通过实际实验和仿真评估表明，我们的对话网络在群体形成方面准确率超过90\%，语音质量比最先进的基线提高了高达8.8分贝，并在移动设备上保持实时性能。一项包含20名参与者的用户研究证实了 CoHear 相比基线的卓越性能和良好可用性。

总之，本论文提出了三种系统，在声学、上下文和社交维度上通过头显可穿戴设备现有传感器实例化用户-环境耦合原理。这些系统不仅提供了详细的实现方案，还为移动感知社区带来了新的见解，展示了耳机如何成为通过环境上下文理解用户的综合平台。
\end{cabstract}
